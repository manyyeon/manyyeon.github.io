Efficient Quantum LDPC Code Discovery via Structural Surrogates and Guided Search

Dayeon Yoo and Milad Marvian

Accurately evaluating candidate quantum LDPC codes can require expensive exact-distance calculations or Monte Carlo decoding simulations. I develop structural surrogates and guided-search methods that identify promising candidates before performing these full evaluations.

Hypergraph-product codes

I use low-weight logical structure to guide greedy and beam searches toward promising modifications of parent Tanner graphs. Across four benchmark families, the resulting codes improve quantum distance by 2–6—including an improvement from dQ = 6 to 12—while reducing logical-error rates by 55%–91%. The search pipeline reduced benchmark runtime by 99.1% relative to random-walk and simulated-annealing baselines.

Bivariate-bicycle codes

I extend the discovery framework to coprime BB codes using sector-aware candidate generation and early rejection of candidates containing low-weight logical operators. The search has identified candidates including [[252, 12, 16]], [[340, 16, 18]], and [[374, 16, 20]].

Evaluation and infrastructure

Candidate codes are validated using exact-distance calculations and BP+OSD Monte Carlo decoding. I built reproducible Slurm-based pipelines for statistical comparisons, failure analysis, and large-scale decoding experiments with up to 5 × 106 trials.
